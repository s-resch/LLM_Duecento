The text is an elegy addressed to a beloved woman who is unresponsive to the speaker's love. The speaker laments the fact that he is unable to serve her due to her aloofness and high-mindedness. He expresses his love and desire to help her, but ultimately acknowledges that he must follow her wishes and leave her to her own devices. The speaker concludes with a plea for his beloved to show mercy and consent to his love, as he believes that it would bring him both joy and honor.
