The text is a sonnet expressing the speaker's love for a woman who does not reciprocate his feelings. He describes the pain and suffering he experiences as a result of her coldness and describes his desire to escape from it. He admits that he is at the mercy of her will and that he will follow her lead even if it means losing his self-respect. He asks the woman to show him mercy and consent to his love, but he is willing to endure any pain or suffering as long as he can be with her. The speaker concludes by saying that he values the knowledge and understanding of love that the woman can provide and hopes that she will reciprocate his feelings.
